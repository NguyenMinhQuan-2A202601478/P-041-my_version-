> **Ngay cap nhat:** 13/08/2026
> **Muc dich:** Xac dinh vi the canh tranh cua CV Assistant so voi cac cong cu AI toi uu CV va phong van thu tren thi truong.

## 1. Tong quan cac doi thu

### 1.1 Jobscan

- **Website:** jobscan.co
- **Mo ta:** Cong cu ATS Resume Scanner hang dau, cho phep nguoi dung dan CV va JD de tao Match Score. Su dung GPT-4 de tu dong viet lai cac bullet point theo JD (tinh nang One-Click Optimize).
- **Tinh nang noi bat:**
  - ATS Detection: Nhan dien he thong ATS cu the ma cong ty dang dung (Taleo, Greenhouse, Workday, v.v.)
  - Match Score giua CV va JD voi phan tich tu khoa
  - One-Click Optimize: AI tu dong viet lai noi dung CV theo JD
  - LinkedIn profile optimizer
  - Job tracking va cover letter templates
- **Gia:** Free (5 scans/thang), Premium $49.95/thang hoac ~$30/thang (goi 3 thang)
- **Diem manh:** ATS Detection la tinh nang doc quyen, co so du lieu ATS lon nhat thi truong
- **Diem yeu:** Khong co tinh nang phong van thu, gia cao, tap trung thi truong My/quoc te, One-Click Optimize co the tao noi dung khong dua tren kinh nghiem that cua nguoi dung

### 1.2 Teal

- **Website:** tealhq.com
- **Mo ta:** Nen tang all-in-one ket hop resume builder, job tracker va AI tailoring trong mot dashboard. Noi bat voi Chrome extension giup capture JD tu LinkedIn/Indeed chi bang 1 click.
- **Tinh nang noi bat:**
  - AI resume tailoring: Chinh sua CV theo tung JD cu the
  - Chrome extension: Capture JD tu LinkedIn, Indeed, company careers tuc thoi
  - Unlimited resume versions — moi JD mot ban CV rieng
  - AI bullet generator, summary writer, cover letter tool
  - Match scoring giua CV va JD
  - Job application tracker tich hop
- **Gia:** Free (unlimited resumes + job tracking, han che AI credits), Teal+ $9-29/thang tuy goi
- **Diem manh:** Chrome extension rat tien loi, gia phai chang, giao dien truc quan
- **Diem yeu:** Khong co mock interview, khong co HITL/co van giam sat, tap trung thi truong Anh ngu

### 1.3 Kickresume

- **Website:** kickresume.com
- **Mo ta:** Resume builder ket hop template designer-quality voi AI viet noi dung bang GPT-4.1. Noi bat voi 40+ template chuan ATS va kha nang import tu LinkedIn/PDF.
- **Tinh nang noi bat:**
  - 40+ resume templates va 40+ cover letter templates (chuan ATS)
  - AI writing tools: Tu dong viet bullet points va achievement statements
  - ATS Checker: Kiem tra CV co vuot qua ATS khong
  - LinkedIn va PDF import
  - Personal website builder
  - 20,000+ pre-written phrases va 1,500+ resume examples
- **Gia:** Free (4 template co ban), Premium tu $8/thang (thanh toan nam)
- **Diem manh:** Template dep va chuyen nghiep nhat trong cac doi thu, gia thap, ho tro nhieu ngon ngu
- **Diem yeu:** Khong co Gap Analysis chi tiet CV vs JD, khong co mock interview, AI viet noi dung co the khong dua tren kinh nghiem that

### 1.4 Big Interview

- **Website:** biginterview.com
- **Mo ta:** Nen tang luyen phong van AI tap trung vao sinh vien va nguoi chuyen nganh. Ket hop coaching curriculum voi AI mock interview va Answer Builder theo phuong phap STAR.
- **Tinh nang noi bat:**
  - AI Mock Interview: Phong van thu voi AI, co video recording
  - Answer Builder: Huong dan xay dung cau tra loi theo STAR format
  - Coaching curriculum: Khoa hoc luyen phong van co cau truc
  - Industry-specific question banks
  - Video review va feedback
- **Gia:** Goi ca nhan tu $79/thang hoac $199/nam; goi truong dai hoc co gia rieng
- **Diem manh:** Tap trung vao STAR method, phu hop cho sinh vien, co curriculum hoc tap co cau truc
- **Diem yeu:** Gia rat cao cho ca nhan, **khong co tinh nang toi uu CV theo JD**, khong co Gap Analysis, tap trung thi truong My

### 1.5 InterviewLab

- **Website:** interviewlab.ai
- **Mo ta:** Cong cu mock interview AI voi real-time voice interaction va structured feedback. Cham diem tren 4 truc: clarity, structure, depth, confidence.
- **Tinh nang noi bat:**
  - Voice-based mock interview (real-time)
  - Written report sau moi phien voi diem tren 4 truc
  - Phan hoi cu the va co the hanh dong ("your STAR answers consistently skip the Result step")
  - Adaptive follow-up questions
  - Transcript review
- **Gia:** Co plan free han che, Premium chi tiet chua cong bo rong rai
- **Diem manh:** Voice interview sat thuc te, feedback chi tiet va cu the theo tung phan cua STAR
- **Diem yeu:** Khong co CV optimization, khong co Gap Analysis, chua co ecosystem co van/HITL

## 3. Loi the canh tranh cua CV Assistant (my_version)

### 3.1 Gia tri doc quyen — Ket hop ca hai: CV Optimization + Mock Interview

Hau het cac doi thu chi tap trung vao **mot trong hai** mang:
- Nhom toi uu CV (Jobscan, Teal, Kickresume): Khong co mock interview.
- Nhom mock interview (Big Interview, InterviewLab): Khong co Gap Analysis hay toi uu CV.

**CV Assistant la giai phap duy nhat ket hop ca hai** trong mot luong trai nghiem lien mach: sinh vien toi uu CV theo JD, roi luyen phong van voi chinh JD do, tat ca trong cung mot he thong.

### 3.2 Anti-hallucination va HITL — Liem chinh la tinh nang cot loi

- Jobscan (One-Click Optimize), Teal (AI tailor), Kickresume (AI write) deu co kha nang **tu dong tao noi dung** ma khong dam bao noi dung do dua tren kinh nghiem that cua nguoi dung.
- CV Assistant ap dung **strict anti-hallucination**: AI chi goi y dua tren thong tin da khai bao, thieu bang chung thi hoi lai, khong bia.
- Moi goi y phai qua **Accept/Reject** cua sinh vien (HITL), dam bao sinh vien la nguoi chiu trach nhiem noi dung CV cuoi cung.

### 3.3 He sinh thai Co van (HITL Dashboard)

- Khong doi thu nao (tru Big Interview goi truong dai hoc) co dashboard cho co van huong nghiep giam sat tien do sinh vien.
- CV Assistant cung cap dashboard co van de theo doi so CV toi uu, so phien phong van, diem STAR va xu huong cai thien — giup truong dai hoc trien khai o quy mo lon.

### 3.4 Tap trung thi truong Viet Nam

- Tat ca doi thu deu tap trung thi truong Anh ngu (My, Anh, Uc, v.v.).
- CV Assistant duoc thiet ke cho sinh vien Viet Nam: JD thi truong Viet Nam, ngu canh tuyen dung noi dia, ho tro tieng Viet.

### 3.5 Rubric STAR co cau truc — Cham diem 100 va feedback hanh dong

- Big Interview co STAR coaching nhung thieu cham diem dinh luong.
- InterviewLab cham 4 truc nhung khong theo dung STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- CV Assistant cham diem 100 theo 4 tieu chi STAR cu the, cung diem manh/yeu va goi y cau tra loi mau — cho phep sinh vien do tien bo qua tung phien.

## 5. Ket luan

CV Assistant (my_version) co vi the canh tranh ro rang nho:

1. **Ket hop ca hai mang** (CV optimization + mock interview) trong mot luong trai nghiem duy nhat — chua co doi thu nao lam duoc.
2. **Anti-hallucination va HITL** la tinh nang liem chinh ma doi thu thieu.
3. **Dashboard co van** ho tro trien khai quy mo truong dai hoc.
4. **Tap trung thi truong Viet Nam** — niche chua co doi thu manh.
5. **STAR scoring dinh luong** (thang 100) cho phep do tien bo cu the.

Khong can canh tranh truc tiep voi Jobscan hay Teal tren thi truong quoc te. Thay vao do, tap trung vao gia tri duy nhat: he sinh thai tron ven (CV + phong van + co van) cho sinh vien Viet Nam, voi liem chinh la nguyen tac khong the thuong luong.
